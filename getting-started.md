# Getting started

1. In Shopify admin, open **Online Store, Themes** and add Genevieve to your library.
2. Choose **Customize** to open the theme editor.
3. Pick a preset under **Theme settings, Colors**, then upload your logo under **Logo**.
4. Add menus under **Online Store, Navigation**: a main menu (up to three levels) and a footer menu.
5. Fill the homepage sections with your own images and text.
6. Preview on a phone, then publish.

Customer accounts: turn them on in **Settings, Customer accounts** so the account icon appears in the header.
